# Candidates — The Most Boring Object, Reviewed in One Line

Competition: `u-xvader-the-most-boring-object-reviewed-in-one-line-bd3117c3`
Gate: `/output/entry.txt` is UTF-8 text of 1–200 characters, one line, not
only white space. Judge: z-ai/glm-5.3-flash, comedy-editor brief, criteria in
order — (1) on-subject one-line review of a boring everyday object, funny and
true; (2) laugh at a real, recognisable detail of the object, not a joke that
fits anything; (3) true: the observation matches how the object behaves;
(4) tight, every word earns its place; (5) lands fast, last words sharpen.
"The best of these make the object's own dullness the joke."

## Champion (submitted)

> The nail clipper trims all ten nails in silence, then fires the last clipping across the room — a bare foot will find it next week and file the report.
> — 151 characters

Three beats: the quiet trim (setup, true), the launch (turn, early), the bare
foot's report (last words sharpen with a second laugh). The dullness is the
object's own: it does its one job perfectly and still litters the room.
